Sticky-Spicy Glazed Sweet Potatoes

Blistered and roasted sweet potatoes are at their best glazed in a sticky-spicy sauce featuring maple syrup, chili paste, vinegar, soy sauce, and miso.

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup pure maple syrup
  • 2 Tbsp. apple cider vinegar
  • 2 Tbsp. hot chili paste (such as sambal oelek)
  • 1 Tbsp. soy sauce
  • 1 Tbsp. white or yellow miso
  • 3 Tbsp. vegetable oil, divided
  • 1½ lb. sweet potatoes (2–3 small), peeled, cut into 1"-thick rounds
  • 3 Tbsp. salted roasted peanuts, preferably cocktail, chopped
  • Sliced pickled sushi ginger, cilantro leaves with tender stems, and lime wedges (for serving)

Instructions

  1. Place a rack in middle of oven; preheat to 375°. Whisk ¼ cup pure maple syrup, 2 Tbsp. apple cider vinegar, 2 Tbsp. hot chili paste, 1 Tbsp. soy sauce, 1 Tbsp. white or yellow miso, 2 Tbsp. vegetable oil, and 2 Tbsp. water in small bowl to combine. Set glaze aside.
  2. Heat remaining 1 Tbsp. vegetable oil in a medium ovenproof skillet, preferably cast iron, over medium. Arrange 1½ lb. sweet potatoes (2–3 small), peeled, cut into 1"-thick rounds, cut side down, in a single layer in skillet (it’s okay if pan seems crowded) and cook, undisturbed, until deep brown, almost charred, underneath, 6–8 minutes. Transfer pan to oven and roast sweet potatoes until tender, 25–35 minutes (a skewer inserted into the center of the largest piece should meet with no resistance).
  3. Return skillet to stovetop over medium heat (be careful; the handle will be very hot). Pour reserved glaze into skillet and cook, turning sweet potatoes occasionally to coat, until glaze is reduced by about a third, about 5 minutes.
  4. Transfer glazed sweet potatoes to a platter, arranging browned side up, and spoon glaze over. Top with 3 Tbsp. salted roasted peanuts, preferably cocktail, chopped, sliced pickled sushi ginger, and cilantro leaves with tender stems. Serve with lime wedges for squeezing over.
